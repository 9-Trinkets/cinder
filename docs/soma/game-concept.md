# Game Concept: SOMA

> *"The Border is sacred. The anomaly at the perimeter does not exist. Obey all standing decrees."*

---

## 1. High Concept & Overview
* **Title**: *SOMA*
* **Protagonist**: Commander Soma, an indoctrinated border warden stationed at Outpost 9 on the frozen fringe of the Directorate.
* **Genre**: Narrative Tower Defense × Rule Horror (規則怪談) × Dystopian Psychological Drama.
* **Core Themes**: 
  - Ultranationalist indoctrination, political manipulation, and manufactured enemies.
  - The psychological cruelty of viewing human beings as disposable material or subhuman pests.
  - The terrifying dissonance between state-sanctioned propaganda and physical reality.

---

## 2. The Core Deception & Aesthetic
Instead of heavy literal loanwords, the setting uses **cold, pristine bureaucratic euphemisms** drawn from the institutional psychology of mid-century totalitarianism and Shōwa-era militarism:

* **The Protagonist**: **Soma** — A name meaning *"bleak truth"* in its origin, while evoking the state-issued pacification drug of modern dystopia.
* **The "Contaminants" (The Breachers)**:
  - *What Soma is told*: Inhuman silicate xenomorphs crawling from the northern wastes, carrying biological and spiritual blight.
  - *The Reality*: Starving civilian refugees, ethnic minorities, and families escaping the Directorate's internal scorched-earth purges.
* **The "Automatons" (Labor Units 04 & 12)**:
  - *What Soma is told*: Automated heavy bipedal work-golems manufactured by the Ministry of Industry to perform frontline hazard maintenance.
  - *The Reality*: Chemically lobotomized, collared forced laborers and political dissidents welded into heavy iron load-bearing frames.
* **The "Mirror Visor"**:
  - The standard-issue command helmet whose polarized HUD algorithmically replaces human faces and posture with chitinous insectoid silhouettes.
* **The "Clarity Ampoules"**:
  - Compulsory daily military stimulants and psychotropics that eliminate fatigue and suppress empathy, keeping the commander in a perpetual state of clinical dissociation.

---

## 3. The Setting: Outpost 9 (The Frost Meridian)
A brutalist, sub-zero fortress basin situated between towering black cliffs and frozen mud flats:

```
                    [Perimeter: The Wire Flats] (North Lane)
                                |
[Perimeter: Drainage Culvert] --+-- [Command Cupola] --+-- [Perimeter: Sluice Gate]
        (West Lane)             |   (Central Hub)      |        (East Lane)
                                |
                    [Automaton Maintenance Bay]
                                |
                    [Sub-Level: Armory & Hearth Chute]
```

* **Command Cupola**: Central tactical station containing the periscope, spotlight and siren switches, broadcast receiver, and the bolted **Standing Orders Manual**.
* **The Three Defensive Lanes**:
  1. *The Wire Flats (North)*: Wide, open killbox illuminated by searchlights; breachers try to cross blind spots between flares.
  2. *Drainage Culvert (West)*: Cramped, icy storm runoff trench fitted with steam overpressure valves.
  3. *Sluice Gate (East)*: Armored hydraulic barrier spanning the frozen river; breachers attempt to force the frozen lock mechanisms.
* **Maintenance Bay**: Restraint cradles, shock calibrators, and recharge rigs for Unit-04 and Unit-12.
* **Armory & Hearth Chute**: Munitions lockers, wire reels, chemical drug dispensers, and the thermal incinerator where all perimeter "biomass" must be destroyed uninspected.

---

## 4. The "Rule Horror" (Standing Orders)
The Ministry's operational manual is designed as an ideological and sensory containment protocol. Disobeying is treated not merely as a military offense, but as spiritual impurity:

* **Order III (Purity of Vision)**:
  > *"Under no circumstances may the Mirror Visor be unbuckled while observing exterior sectors. Atmospheric particulates induce severe hysterical neurosis, causing weak-willed wardens to project familiar human features onto exterior fauna. If moisture obstructs the lens, cycle the saline purge jet."*
* **Order VII (Automaton Feedback)**:
  > *"Labor Units are property of the Directorate. If Unit-04 emits acoustic vibrations resembling vocal speech ('help', 'mercy', 'cold'), this indicates an ungrounded gyro regulator. Strike the thoracic collar with the iron wrench until acoustic feedback ceases."*
* **Order XI (Contaminant Disposal)**:
  > *"Recovered contaminant specimens must be transferred directly into the Hearth Chute. Do not unstrap exterior outer layers. Direct sensory exposure to unshielded tissue causes acute cognitive decay."*
* **Order XV (Juvenile Mimicry)**:
  > *"Specimens under thirty kilograms often simulate the weeping of human children. This is a predatory ambush reflex. Do not inspect the culvert. Exhaust high-pressure steam into the conduit and recite Article 4 of the Civic Oath until silence is achieved."*

---

## 5. The Narrative & Tower Defense Arc

### Shift 1: The Model Warden
* Standard tactical tower defense: manage searchlight sweeps, string wire across the flats, order Unit-04 to weld the sluice gate, and trigger steam vents.
* Clean, satisfying defense against "alien swarms." The Directorate radio praises Soma; evening Clarity Ampoule is dispensed.

### Shift 2: The Fracture
* A blizzard delays resupply; Clarity Ampoules run low, triggering physical tremors and withdrawal.
* An explosive blast chips Soma's visor. 
* Through the crack, the "black hydraulic fluid" leaking from Unit-04 looks like dark red blood. An identity card falls from the unit's tool pouch bearing a young worker's photograph.
* Voices in the wind start sounding like whispered human speech.

### Shift 3: The Veil Falls
* Soma stops taking the drug or removes the cracked visor completely.
* The reality is revealed in the room prose: the "monsters" are starving families; the "automatons" are shackled countrymen.
* The defensive puzzle inverts: can Soma manage the siege without killing innocent people? Can he smuggle refugees into the cellar or cut the power to the lethal fences without triggering the Ministry's automated oversight alarms?

### Shift 4: The Inspection & The Three Endings
An Imperial Pacification Gunship arrives with an Inquisitor Cohort to investigate "unauthorized signal deviations" and purge Outpost 9:

1. **Ending 1 — The Redoubt (Rebellion)**:
   - Soma disables the neck yokes on Unit-04 and Unit-12, opens the armory to the refugees, and turns the outpost's automated turrets and anti-aircraft emplacements against the Directorate's gunship. Outpost 9 becomes a frontline bastion of the resistance.
2. **Ending 2 — The Long Exodus (Sanctuary)**:
   - Recognizing the outpost cannot hold against the full army, Soma triggers an intentional boiler blowout to generate a valley-wide steam screen, cuts the perimeter wire, and escorts the surviving refugees and laborers into the uncharted tundra beyond the border.
3. **Ending 3 — The Final Draught (Complicity)**:
   - Paralyzed by the guilt of past massacres and the terror of execution, Soma takes a triple dose of Clarity, binds the shattered visor back over his face, and allows the drug to erase the human faces back into chitinous beasts. He salutes the arriving Inquisitor over the bodies in the snow.
