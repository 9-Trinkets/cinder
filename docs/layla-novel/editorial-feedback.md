# Editorial Feedback: 《蕾拉》(Layla)

Scope: prologue through epilogue (about 92k characters), plus README, 角色語氣指南 and 翻譯對照表. 服飾與世界觀階級指南 and 紋印與組合語言設定指南 were not reviewed. Line references (`chapter:line`) point to the manuscript as of this review.

## Overall

A strong concept with a coherent spine. Each layer is a different board game, each flashback carries one lesson that unlocks that chapter, and each chapter adds one rune. The ending hook, a tactical knife whose grip fits a broken little finger, is well chosen. The weaknesses are in the back half. The middle runs on repeated beats at low stakes, and the book's central moral claim is undercut by its own chapters 8 and 11.

## What works

- **Ch 3 is the strongest chapter.** The mancala-as-fire-elemental puzzle is clever, and "放開" carries the grandfather's lesson. Board-game logic and emotion are the same thing here.
- **Ch 2's "棄著" ending.** Winning by making the king resign is a good idea, and it's why Layla refuses to kill the queen.
- **The lamp is well planted.** It runs from 「燈呢？」(ch 2) through the 石守 resonance (ch 6) to the awakening (ch 11). The reveal is earned.
- **Ch 5's 「我是人」 beat.** The ankle and pinky read as human evidence on a first pass and as engineering evidence on a reread. Ch 13's knife pays this off.
- **The ch 8 enemy-POV prologue** is fresh and has real menace.
- **Ch 10's cult worldbuilding.** The call-and-response, the 30° bow and the 悔罪室 are the best new atmosphere since the village.
- **The grandfather's lessons as chapter keys.** The chapter-by-chapter lessons (留口氣, 執黑先行, 王認輸則不流血, 放手) are a good structural device, and they stay useful after the twist (see issue 3).

## Priority issues

### 1. Ch 8's golem death contradicts ch 12's moral claim

- In ch 8 Layla puts her hand on the black golem's shoulder and says 「截斷衝擊」. That is arguably an order to die for her.
- In ch 11 she learns every golem is a person (「採礦勞工十二名──繫入石魔像哨兵」).
- In ch 12 the operator praises her for refusing to let any piece die for her.
- **Opportunity:** make this the engine of the finale. Her refusal in ch 12 would hit harder if it is penance for the golem. As written, the book never notices the contradiction.
- Related: Layla gets 縛紋 in ch 11 and uses it only on Siraj and Nur. Her own companions are never awakened, named or mourned. The glossary already has Orin, Mari, Ferid and Hana, and none appear in the text.

### 2. The ch 13 emotional peak is in the wrong voice

- Zayd's reunion with his parents (13:162) is one summary paragraph in omniscient narration. Layla is in the real world and cannot see it. That breaks the strict first-person rule in the README.
- It is also the payoff for the whole book. Give it a scene, ideally in ch 12 before the exit.
- 13:158 says Nur "信守了她對我的承諾", but no such promise exists in ch 12.
- Nobody tells Elder Rashid or Yasmin the truth about Zayd's parents, so Rashid's guilt arc is left hanging.

### 3. The ch 12 reveal is delivered, not discovered

- The reveal arrives as a data dump (代碼流, 「博弈模型・編號七號」). Layla goes from denial to anger to defiance in about five paragraphs, with no silent beat.
- The finale abandons the book's premise. Every layer is a board game, then the last test is a 15-minute hide-and-seek in a maze.
- There is an unused callback: the ch 0 lesson 「給人家留口氣」. A seventh game where refusing the sacrifice is the winning move would honor the whole structure.
- Plant one or two more mid-book doubts. The best fair-play clue is the ch 11 line about a feeling 「未曾在任何棋理與對弈算度中被記錄過」. More like it are needed.
- **The grandfather is fake, and the book should use that.** Ch 12 states plainly that the "elders" are training data from millions of human games (12:75–77). The flashbacks therefore come from real human players, even though the grandfather as a person never existed. That is the same argument the book makes about pain (「痛苦也是加權參數」, then Astrid's rebuttal). Ch 12 could say it directly: the lessons were real even if the grandfather was not. A final flashback, or Layla recalling the lessons after the reveal, would tie the identity twist to the book's thesis.

### 4. Mid-book stakes collapse

- After ch 3, almost every fight ends in 10 seconds or less (ch 5 says so outright). The golems do the work, and Layla loses nothing until ch 8.
- Ch 9 is the weakest case. It is tower defense told as a summary. Fane and Thorin are names with no personality, and Lady Sylvan concedes after one speech.
- Ch 10's "find the witch" puzzle is four suspects and one obvious hairpin. The guides promise werewolf-style deduction. Make it harder, for example by having Harun's search strip everyone's accessories.

### 5. Open threads and operator inconsistencies

- **Open threads after the twist.** Since the grandfather is explicitly fake, the remaining questions are:
  - Whose hand and little finger was the knife (13:194–198) shaped for?
  - The operator's 「到底是誰，在妳的核心裡留下了這種多餘的執念？」 (12:375) is asked and never answered.
  - If these are sequel hooks, make that legible. If not, resolve them.
- **The operator's props.** He smokes and flicks a lighter (2:400, 3:339–349) but drinks tea in ch 12.
- **The 老人 leak.** He is described as 蒼老 and 老人 in 1:400 and 6:275–289, which leaks the "old man" identity the README wanted buried until ch 12.
- **Speech length.** The handler guide says his lines run 1–8 characters. Ch 5, ch 6 and ch 9 give him paragraph-long speeches.

## Prose and voice

- **Repetition counts across the manuscript:** 宛如 ×49, 驟然 ×35, 死死 ×42, 「不是…而是」 ×29, 猛地 ×20, 彷彿 ×21.
- **Idioms that clash with the no-武俠 rule:** 泰山壓頂 (5:333, 9:143), 排山倒海, 萬鈞.
- **Duplicated simile:** 爆米花 describes the fire sprites' appearance in both 5:100 and 9:73, and is tonally off.
- **Layla's voice.**
  - The guide wants a dry, wry, teaching-a-friend voice that never barks orders. The chapters have her shouting 「盾牆！」 and 「變陣！」.
  - Her wit (the 「這人講話怎麼這麼累」 gag) disappears after ch 1. Bring it back, especially at emotional beats.
- **Dropped motif.** The chapter-ending "第X局終了" runs only through ch 6. Either finish it (chs 10–12 would suit) or cut it deliberately.

- **彻底:** 5:116. Simplified character; should be 徹底.
- **Typos:**
  - 5:15 泊泊 should be 汩汩.
  - 5:345 陰鶩 should be 陰騖.
  - 6:261 「擴展展開」 is a doubled word.

- **消耗了四點魔力儲備:** 5:90. This is the stat-speak purged in earlier commits.
- **薩克拉:** 11:41. A third name mid-sentence, against the glossary's rule to use 石守 until the reveal, then 西拉傑.
- **內門:** 7:229. A literal use, but on the banned list. Use 內側門.
- **Full names without interpuncts:** 妮薇歐森, 艾莉絲卡諾瓦科娃 and so on (9:219–229). Use 妮薇·歐森 or drop the surnames, as ch 7 does. The glossary has the same flaw.

## Documentation upkeep

- 角色語氣指南 still describes Lady Sylvan's third-person POV, and its §2.7 and §3 are out of order and duplicated.
- The README's retcon plan for Harun and Einar (§5.9) is unchecked.
- 作品簡介 describes a masked ruler "高居第七層聖堂", but the ruler turns out to be an unmasked old man.
- 翻譯對照表 still lists 傳送代幣, which the style rules ban.

## Suggested order of work

1. Reconcile the ch 8 golem death with ch 12's moral claim, and use 縛紋 on the golems.
2. Dramatize Zayd's reunion in-scene and in first person.
3. Rework ch 12's reveal with a fair-play lead-in and a game-shaped final test, and use the "fake grandfather, real lessons" idea.
4. Raise mid-book stakes (ch 9 first) and harden the ch 10 deduction.
5. Fix the continuity table.
6. Apply the copy-edit list, then trim the repeated phrases.

## New issues introduced by the edits

1. **Golem names leak before the reveal.**
   - Ch 9 narrates 法瑞德 (9:71, 9:101), 瑪莉 and 哈娜 (9:127, 9:291), and 歐林 (9:113) in Layla's first-person voice.
   - Ch 11:64 says she learned Orin's name 「直到此刻」. This spoils the ch 11 reveal and contradicts it.
   - Use 黑石魔像 and 白石魔像 until ch 11.
2. **Ch 12 contradicts itself about previous candidates.**
   - 12:73 says every candidate who reached layer 6 was torn apart by the dragon.
   - 12:37 and 12:273 say previous candidates reached this hall, sacrificed allies and passed.
   - Pick one version.
3. **Layla's own ch 9 trap undercuts ch 12.** Ch 9 has her flood the courtyard with oil and set fire to about 100 men. The text calls it 「殺戮熔爐」 and 「殺戮區」. Ch 12 praises her for building no slaughterhouse and for 「給人家留口氣」. Either soften the ch 9 language or have ch 12 acknowledge it.
4. **The lamp is in two places.** Siraj carries it (12:593–643, 13:172), but 13:164 has Zayd 「手裡緊緊抱著那盞黃銅礦燈」.
5. **The earlier flashbacks don't match the clues ch 12 now relies on.**
   - Ch 12 says the chess hand was 白皙瘦削 with ink scent (12:125). Ch 2's flashback has a 粗糙而溫暖的大手, and ch 0 also says 粗糙.
   - Ch 4 (added line) says two hands. Ch 12 says three.
   - Ch 12:103 says the third memory was a 「木製長廊」. Ch 3 has a window with a wooden board.
   - Ch 2 has 熱紅茶, while ch 12 anchors on 麥茶.
   - The clue only works if the flashbacks actually show it.
6. **English in the prose, against the style rule.**
   - `Project Cinder`, `Shatranj`, `Designation: Layla`, `Mentor Archetype Narrative Scaffold`, and `Minimax` ×2 (12:149–267, 12:535).
   - Ch 13's terminal text (`LAYLA-07`, `SECTOR_7_NUR`).
   - The README and glossary say the body text is pure Chinese. If these are deliberate document or screen text, make that an exception in the style guide.
7. **Ch 12 and 13 slips.**
   - 12:651 is garbled: 「艾納咬了眨眼眶裡的淚水」.
   - 13:162 says 「她們必須留在…」 about Nur and Siraj, which should be 他們.
   - 13:164 has 「不再冰冷的風雪」 on layer 4, which has no snow.
8. **Banned or off-style terms in the new text.**
   - 睡穴 (10:244) is in the 點穴 family that is banned.
   - Stat-speak is back: 「勝率跌破了百分之五」 (9:143), 「機率…百分之百」 (10:330), 「生還的機率」 (5:227, 11:53).
   - 7:197 has 「跳動的精確數值」, which is HUD-like.
9. **The Halfling change has loose ends.**
   - Halflings with 「尖細耳廓」 (10:67, 10:430) read as elves, which is confusing next to 妖精.
   - Olva's escape from Harun's search is never explained.
   - Harun is never resolved: 11:116 is his last mention.
10. **The operator's nature is now ambiguous.** Ch 12 no longer says he is an AI. He only says 「數百年」 and 「該死的輪班」. That is fine if intentional, but the README still calls him an AI.
11. **Doc mismatches.**
    - README §5.9 now marks the retcon as done (「已完成」). The text doesn't back that up: ch 4, ch 5 and ch 7 show no cult-discipline traces for Harun or Einar.
    - The README's ch 12 summary says 「一千四百萬盤」, but the text says 「十四萬兩千套」.
