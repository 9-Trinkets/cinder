# Noa: story spine for the first act (draft)

**Superseded by `hal-story-spine.md`; kept for reference.**

Companion to `session-design.md`. Fully authored, no LLM. Built on the secrets already written for Noa in `content/isla/locales/en/actors.json` and `beats.json`. Not playtested.

## 1. The shape of her story

A child who learned to be invisible so nothing would break, and an adult who is excellent at the first hour and gone by the second.

Each secret moves one step closer to her:

| # | Secret | Whose story it sounds like | Closer to her |
|---|--------|----------------------------|---------------|
| S1 | The silent piano | her mother's | the first hint that wanting costs something |
| S2 | The three months | something that happened to her | she was sent away and never told why |
| S3 | The roommate | her present life | the same pattern is still running |
| S4 | The core fear | herself | what is under the pattern |

The order is also the order of depth, so the ladder and the story agree. The old "invisible child" secret is folded into S3: the pattern she learned at fourteen is the reason the last relationship ended.

## 3. The act, bid by bid

Ordinary bids are 1 point each (first try only). Secrets are 2 points each and score only if held. Total available: 8 + 4x2 = **16 points**.

### Ordinary bids

| # | When | Type | Her line | Why it matters |
|---|------|------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Arrival | Invitation | "Is there something I'm supposed to do?" | she wants to be a good patient |
| 2 | Intake | Feeling | "People say I'm easy to be around. I take it as a compliment." | plant for S4 |
| 3 | Intake | Feeling | "I'm very good at the first hour of anything." | plant for S3 |
| 4 | Serve | Deflection | "Oh, please don't go to any trouble." | plant for S4 |
| 5 | Stop 1 (after spread 3) | Feeling | "It's a nice house. Very well run. Everyone gets what they need." | the book is landing |
| 6 | Stop 1 | Invitation | "Is there more?" | she wants to continue |
| 7 | Stop 2 (after spread 5) | Invitation | "Why didn't the house ask her to stay?" | she asks you what she cannot ask herself |
| 8 | Stop 4 (after spread 9) | Feeling | "It's so quiet when the lights go out." | opens the way to S3 |

### Secrets

Each is an Opening bid. **Held** means Reflect her words or Wait. Anything else is **fumbled**: she gives a deflecting line, the fact is known, and nothing scores.

**S1: The silent piano.** After spread 5 (the guest stands in the doorway and the house says "Safe travels!").
- *Telling:* "My mother had a piano. She didn't get into the orchestra, and after that she stopped playing. It just sat in our living room. I was ten. I remember thinking: this is what happens if you want something too much."
- *Held:* her hand rests flat on the table as if on keys. "I still dust it when I visit. Nobody plays it." Reframe: wanting something and not saying it.
- *Fumbled:* "It's just a piano." She turns back to the book.

**S2: The three months.** After spread 7 (a child asks where its mother is, and the house offers soup instead of an answer).
- *Telling:* "When I was fourteen, she stopped getting out of bed. I was sent to my aunt's. Nobody said why. When I came back, we never talked about it."
- *Held:* she lets the cup sit, saucer crooked. "I think I decided it was my job not to ask." Reframe: silence as a role she took.
- *Fumbled:* "It was fine. She got better."

**S3: The roommate.** After spread 9 (the lights go out).
- *Telling:* "My ex said I was an excellent roommate who didn't need them. I thought it was a compliment for about a week."
- *Held:* "They were asking whether I was in there." A pause. "I was doing what I learned at fourteen. Performing fine, so they wouldn't leave." Reframe: the pattern is still running.
- *Fumbled:* "Anyway. I am a good roommate. That's just true."

**S4: The core fear.** After spread 12, the last page. Only if S3 was held.
- *Telling:* "If anyone really saw me, the version that doesn't have it together, they'd leave. That's what happened."
- *Held:* a long silence. "I don't know if there's anyone under there. I'd like to find out." (Keep it this modest. No neat resolution.)
- *Fumbled:* a small laugh. "That's probably more than you asked for." She thanks you politely.

### The gate

She tells a secret only if you have earned at least **60% of the points available so far** when its moment arrives. S4 also needs S3 held. This replaces the ladder rungs for gating and is simpler to explain; the "hurt drops her a rung" rule becomes "a hurt counts as unmet and she is more guarded". Thresholds are guesses.

## 4. Closing and review

**Closing menu** offers only lines she actually said, so it depends on how far you got:
- "It's a nice house. Very well run." (surface)
- "I'm very good at the first hour of anything." (a plant)
- "I think I decided it was my job not to ask." (S2 held)
- **"They were asking whether I was in there."** (S3 held: the real insight)

If she never reached S3, the real line is not on the menu and the closing can only be a lesser one.

**Review facts** (each picks an authored line): S1 held, S2 held, S3 held, S4 held, book fit, closing line chosen. A held secret earns a warm line in the review, an unheld one earns silence.

## 5. Open questions

1. **The ex's gender.** The pack is inconsistent ("their parting words", "she told me", and "he" in the table read). The spine uses "my ex" and "they". Decide.
2. **Number of stops.** This spine has five (after spreads 3, 5, 7, 9 and 12). The design said three or four. Merge bid 8 into the stop 3 or 4 if it feels long.
3. **Near-miss book.** Proposal: it reaches S1 and maybe S2 at most, and the rest stay surface bids.
4. **Is S4 reachable in one act?** It needs S3 held and a high score. That may be right for a first act and is good for replay, but it may feel out of reach.
5. **Gate threshold.** 60% is a guess. Test in the prototype.
6. **Content care.** The stories are everyday and intimate (a silent piano, being sent away, a roommate remark). The prototype should stay at that level and not drift toward graphic trauma.
