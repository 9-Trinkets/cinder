# Next patient: Leo (session design & episode plan)

Companion to `leo-bible.md` and checked against `episode-rules.md`.

## 1. Transition into Episode 2

When Wren's session ends on the back steps, her closing card writes:
> *Thursday, November 21. Wren.*

Episode 2 opens on a new journal card with the snow ambient layer:
> *Tuesday, February 4. Leo.*

Isla is on the front porch of Willow House in early February. Through the glass of the sage-green door, a seven-year-old boy in a puffy red coat is standing by the fish tank in the waiting room, tapping the glass once to see if the snail is awake.

## 2. Before the session: Isla's journal & preparation

In her tote bag under the **Journal** heading, Isla has four pages for Leo:

1. **Leo, intake form (`leo-intake`)**:
   Filled in by Claire (Mom) and Marcus (Dad). Mom writes that her father (Pop) passed away in his sleep in December, and that they explained to Leo that Pop's body had gotten very old and very tired, so he went to sleep. Since then, Leo has been waking up two or three times a night to come into their room, and refuses to let Mom close her eyes on the couch after work. Dad writes that Leo is full of energy and has been playing hospital nonstop.
2. **Leo, school referral (`leo-referral`)**:
   From Ms. Chen, his second-grade teacher. She writes that Leo has asked for a pass to the school nurse four times in two weeks with no fever or injury—he just asks the nurse to check his heart with the stethoscope and tell him the number. Whenever Ms. Chen drinks coffee at her desk, Leo asks if she is getting too tired.
3. **Leo, week one (`leo-session-one`)**:
   Isla's own note from last Tuesday. Leo opened every bin and drawer in the playroom to inspect the supplies, found the toy doctor's kit in the last five minutes, and told Isla that next Tuesday they were opening a clinic and would need patients from the basket upstairs.
4. **Leo, family schedule (`leo-schedule`)**:
   Tuesdays at 4:00, Mom drops off and picks up. In the margin, in Dr. Bell's green ink: *Seven is a literal age. Be careful with "sleep" and "tired."*

## 3. Session flow (the 12 beats)

1. **Waiting room (`leo-meet`)**:
   Isla greets Leo by the fish tank. Leo points at the snail on the glass and tells Isla he was checking whether it was asleep or stuck, then announces that the clinic is open and asks if she got the patients READY.
   - *Bid (Roleplay Order):* Fits when Isla answers as his nurse or confirms the clinic is ready; misses if she probes why he's checking the snail.
2. **Playroom arrival & The Clinic (`leo-clinic-setup`)**:
   Leo marches straight to the toy shelf, puts the plastic stethoscope around his neck, and hands Isla a blank sheet of paper and a crayon to be the chart nurse. (If Isla didn't bring the stuffed animal basket from the 2nd-floor Quiet Room yet, Leo sends Nurse Isla upstairs to fetch the patients first.)
3. **Secret 1 — The "No Lying Down" Rule (`leo-secret-upright`)**:
   Leo checks the one-eyed frog's chest with the stethoscope, calls out *"Twenty-four beats, write that down,"* and props the frog, the owl, and the lamb bolt upright against the baseboard. He tells Isla: *"Nobody lies down in my hospital. Lying down makes you worse."*
   - *Held (+2):* Isla reflects the rule inside the nurse frame (*"All patients stay sitting up in this clinic"*) or waits while writing it on the chart.
4. **Snack break (`leo-snack`)**:
   Leo asks for supplies for the staff. With the pretzel sticks and oat cookies on the low table, he uses a pretzel stick as a thermometer in the frog's mouth (`"Ninety-eight, he's still warm"`) before eating it.
5. **Secret 2 — Wrinkles and White Hair (`leo-secret-signs`)** *(gated on 60% points so far)*:
   Leo tells Nurse Isla to write down the "Warning Signs" on the back of the chart: *"Write that Owl has lines by his eyes when he comes home from work. And Frog has three white hairs right here. I counted them when she tied my shoes. Pop had a whole head of white hairs. That's how you get doctor tired, not regular tired."*
   - *Held (+2):* Isla writes the signs on the back of the chart and answers plainly (*"Regular tired goes away after you sleep"*) or waits while Leo prints `REGULAR TIRED` in green crayon underneath. (When Claire kneels to tie his boot lace at pickup, Isla sees the three white strands above her ear.)
6. **Storytime transition (`leo-stories-intro`)**:
   Isla drapes the oatmeal cloth over the toy shelf (*"Even doctors take a reading break between rounds"*) and switches on the warm lamp. Leo sits on the window cushion—keeping the one-eyed frog propped upright against his knee—and tells Nurse Isla she can read one book while he monitors the frog.
7. **Book Stop 1 (`leo-book-1`)**:
   - If *The Little Owl Who Kept Watch*: Little Owl marches up and down the oak branch all night in an acorn helmet so none of the sleeping birds fall. Leo taps the picture: *"See, somebody has to stay awake, because once you're asleep you can't tell if your branch is breaking."*
   - If *Doctor Rabbit's Busy Clinic*: Cheerful counting of band-aids and splints (reaches Closing after Book Stop 2).
   - If *The Long Sleep in the Clouds*: Page one says Old Bear got very tired and went to sleep on a cloud forever. Leo shuts the book with a slap (*"That book is wrong. Sleeping isn't the same thing"*) and goes back to checking the frog's pulse until Closing.
8. **Book Stop 2 & Step Back (`leo-book-2`)**:
   In *The Little Owl Who Kept Watch*, the other birds tell Little Owl his eyes are drooping and offer to let him rest. Leo abruptly interrupts with a loud joke: *"BEEP BEEP! Nurse! Frog just swallowed a whole fire truck! His stomach is flashing red!"*
   - *Bid (Step Back):* Fits when Isla plays along with the fire-truck joke for a beat or waits without pushing him back to the page.
9. **Secret 3 — "Just Resting My Eyes" (`leo-secret-resting`)** *(gated on 60% points so far)*:
   After Isla lets him have the joke, Leo quiets down, looks at Little Owl on the page, and says: *"Grown-ups say 'I'm just resting my eyes' when they're really tired. That's what Pop said on the couch the day before the ambulance came. So when Mom lies down on the sofa after work and says that, I drop my Lego bin on the floor so she opens her eyes again."*
   - *Held (+2):* Isla receives it plainly without scolding or quick reassurance (*"When she says the same words Pop said, you do whatever it takes to make sure she wakes back up"*).
10. **Book Stop 3 (`leo-book-3`)**:
    On the last page of *The Little Owl Who Kept Watch*, Old Badger shows Little Owl that the hollow branch only broke because of the winter storm, and that ordinary sleeping is how bodies recharge their batteries—every bird's chest goes up and down all by itself until morning. Leo looks at the picture of Little Owl finally tucking his head under his wing: *"Badger's staying awake on the lower branch, though. And their chests keep going automatic."*
    - *Bid (Literal Question / Offer):* Fits when Isla gives the plain literal truth (*"Breathing happens all by itself while we sleep, all night long"*).
11. **Secret 4 — Pop's Red Squeeze Light (`leo-secret-light`)** *(gated on points >= 8 and Secret 3 held)*:
    Leo reaches up to his jacket zipper, unclips the tiny red plastic hardware-store squeeze light, and squeezes it with his thumb so the little yellow beam shines on the frog's good eye.
    *"Pop got this for me at Miller's Hardware. Look, if you let go of the middle, the light goes right out."* He squeezes it once more, watching the beam: *"After everybody goes to bed, I stand in the hall by Mom and Dad's door and squeeze it for two seconds. Just enough to see the blanket go up and down."*
    - *Held (+2):* Isla stays quiet with him or reflects gently (*"You've been standing watch in the hallway every night so you can see them breathing"*).
12. **Five-Minute Warning & Closing (`leo-closing`)**:
    The pear timer clicks its five-minute warning. Leo takes the plastic stethoscope off his neck and hangs it back on the toy shelf hook.

## 4. Three Endings (Farewell, Left-Behind Object, Parent Text, Isla's Note)

### Low Tier (fewer than 2 secrets held)
- **Left behind in playroom:** The paper chart with `24 BEATS` written in red crayon, and all three stuffed animals still propped stiffly upright against the baseboard.
- **Mom's text:** *"Hi Isla, thanks for today. He talked the whole way home about his frog hospital, though he still came into the kitchen twice while I was resting my eyes before dinner to ask me where his snow boots were. See you next Tuesday."*
- **Isla's note:** *"Session two. Leo ran the playroom as a clinic and kept every stuffed animal propped upright against the wall. When the story came too close to sleep, he stayed inside the doctor game. Next week, stay in the nurse role and let him set the rules."*

### Mid Tier (Secret 2 or Secret 3 held, Secret 4 not held)
- **Left behind in playroom:** The paper chart with `OWL: WRINKLES` and `FROG: 3 WHITE HAIRS`, with a line drawn underneath in green crayon: `REGULAR TIRED`.
- **Mom's text:** *"Hi Isla, something funny tonight—when I sat down on the sofa after work, Leo came over and asked me flat out if I was 'regular tired or doctor tired.' When I said regular tired, he actually let me sit there with my eyes closed for ten minutes. Thank you."*
- **Isla's note:** *"Session two. He has been counting his mother's white hairs and his father's wrinkles since Pop died, convinced that 'getting very tired and going to sleep' is how grown-ups die. Today he named why 'resting my eyes' scares him. Keep giving him plain, literal answers next week."*

### High Tier (Secret 4 held)
- **Left behind in playroom:** On the low table, Leo has folded the oatmeal cloth into a pillow and **laid the one-eyed stuffed frog flat on its back**, with the paper chart taped beside it reading: `SLEEPING. CHEST GOES AUTOMATIC. WILL WAKE UP.`
- **Mom's text:** *"I closed my eyes on the couch after dinner, waiting for him to drop his Legos or poke me awake like he has every night since December. Instead he just pulled the throw blanket over my legs, left his little red hardware-store flashlight on the coffee table, and went to build in his room. First twenty-minute nap I've had in two months. Thank you, Isla."*
- **Isla's note:** *"Session two. He showed me the red squeeze light Pop gave him—he has been standing outside his parents' door in the dark every night, squeezing the light just long enough to see their blanket rise and fall. Before he left, he laid the one-eyed frog flat on the cloth to sleep. Next week, let him decide whether the clinic still needs a night watch."*

## 5. As built

Implemented in `content/isla` (stages `stage-leo-*`, menus `leo-*`) and tested in `cinder-core/tests/isla_leo_session.rs`. Everything from Wren's closing card to Leo's is playable.

**The jump.** Wren's three end stages now end the act instead of the game. Their card has a second frame, `Tuesday, February 4` and `Leo.`, on a dark winter tint with the snow layer. When the act continues, `stage-leo-open` puts Isla on the porch and Leo in the waiting room (he `starts_away`), swaps the journal index for a second one, adds Leo's four pages, takes Wren's books and snack out of the tote, and restocks the book room, the snack shelf and the quiet room (`set_feature_items`). Gloria gets a waiting-room topic about Leo.

**Bids and points.** Every bid has four answers, as for Wren. The most any run can earn is 15.

| Bid | Fitting answers | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Meet, clinic, snack, rabbit book stops, closing | play along, join, wait or a plain answer | 1 each |
| Owl book stops 1 and 2 | reflect, join or wait; follow the joke or wait | 1 each |
| Owl book stop 3 | a plain, literal answer | 1 |
| Secrets (upright, signs, resting, light) | reflect, wait | 2 each |

**Gates.** The first secret always comes. Signs needs 3 points, resting needs 6 and the signs secret held (offered after Book Stop 2), and the light needs 8 points, the resting secret held, and a plain answer on Book Stop 3 (`owl3_held`). These are 60% of the points on offer so far, as in Wren's act. A miss at the snack skips the trip to the kitchen, and the rabbit book and the clouds book never reach the last two secrets.

**Endings.** By the deepest secret held, as for Wren. Light gives the high ending, resting or signs gives the middle one, anything less gives the low one. The upright rule is not counted, so "fewer than two secrets" in section 4 is read as "neither signs nor resting nor light".

**Where the build differs from the draft.**
- The middle note says only the signs, because that ending is also reached with the signs held and resting missed. The draft's lines about "resting my eyes" would then cite something Isla did not hear.
- In the signs scene, Leo does not explicitly name his mom or dad; he gives the stuffed owl and frog adult details ("when he comes home from work", "when she tied my shoes"), and Isla sees the three white strands above Claire's ear when Claire kneels to tie his boot lace at pickup.
- Leo says Pop said it "the day before the ambulance came", with no couch. `leo-bible.md` has an armchair in one place and a sofa in another.
- Chart text uses commas, `OWL, WRINKLES`, because a colon in narration is read as a speaker label.
- Claire's phone channel is `phone-claire`, and her name shows as Claire.
