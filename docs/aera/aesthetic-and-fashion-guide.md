# Aesthetic, Architecture & Fashion Guide: AERA

> *"A sharehouse is not just a backdrop; it is a glass terrarium designed to expose the friction between six different aesthetic worlds."*

## 1. Architectural Materiality: The Da’an Townhouse

The sharehouse is a meticulously renovated 1970s three-story Taiwanese townhouse (*tian-cuo* 透天厝) tucked deep inside the leafy lanes of Da’an District. The design preserves historic Taiwanese residential craft while incorporating Japanese minimalist joinery and modern industrial finishes.

### Room-by-Room Architectural Breakdown

#### Ground Floor: The Public Stage
* **Entrance Genkan (落塵玄關)**: 
  * Sunken polished grey concrete. A clean bench of reclaimed camphor wood. 
  * Rows of clean matching leather guest slippers alongside the cast's daily shoes: Kai's worn Converse, Ren's oiled kitchen clogs, Sumi's barefoot leather sandals, Minh Chau's platform tabi sneakers, Noah's cleated cycling shoes.
* **Open Chef's Kitchen & Dining Room (開放廚房與長桌餐廳)**:
  * Massive central stainless-steel island with commercial-grade gas burners, overhead pot rack with copper pans, and dual sunken sinks.
  * Long 10-seater dining table crafted from a single slab of solid Taiwanese cypress (*hinoki* 檜木). Overhead hang three low-wattage fluted ceramic pendant lamps (warm 2400K light).
* **Sunken Living Lounge & Courtyard (下沉客廳與天井中庭)**:
  * Steps down into a sunken floor lined with original deep green-and-white flecked terrazzo (*mo-shi-zi*).
  * Low modular linen sofa in slate grey. No television. Bookshelves lined with art monographs, film stills, and architecture books.
  * Floor-to-ceiling iron-framed glass accordion doors open completely into the **Interior Courtyard**, where a 50-year-old banyan tree and giant elephant-ear leaves rise against raw concrete walls. Sumi's small earthen dye pots soak under the banyan shade.

#### Second Floor: The Introspective Domain
* **The Studio / Library (設計工作室與書房)**:
  * Glass-walled room overlooking the courtyard. Contains Ae-ra’s dual-monitor editing station, Noah’s architectural drafting table, Minh Chau's dress form with half-draped fabrics, and a vintage Marantz turntable.
* **The Girls’ Room (女生臥室, 3-Bed Dormitory)**:
  * Elevated tatami platform holding three individual low linen futon mattresses: Ae-ra's neat charcoal duvet, Sumi's hand-dyed botanical woven throw, and Minh Chau's vibrant silk cushions.
  * Sliding partitions of frosted rice paper and begonia-embossed glass (*hai-tang-hua*).
  * High industrial clothing rack displaying Minh Chau’s deconstructed jackets, Ae-ra’s oversized minimalist wool coats, and Sumi’s natural linen tunics.

#### Third Floor: The Elements & The Guys’ Dorm
* **The Guys’ Room (男生臥室, 3-Bed Dormitory)**:
  * Polished terrazzo floor with industrial steel-frame beds: Ren's neatly folded indigo quilts, Noah's military-grade taut linens, Kai's messy tangle of duvets and loose guitar picks.
  * Wall racks holding Noah’s matte-black carbon-fiber aero road bike, Kai’s vintage tweed guitar amplifier, and Ren’s culinary notebooks.
* **Rooftop Terrace & Laundry Pavilion (頂樓露台與天台洗衣亭)**:
  * Weathered teak decking under a corrugated glass roof. Exposed aluminum ducting.
  * Two heavy industrial washing machines humming late at night, smelling of cedar detergent.
  * String of warm tungsten festoon lights over a pair of Acapulco chairs, daybed, and concrete planters filled with wild rosemary, mint, and Thai holy basil. Views of the misty silhouette of Taipei 101 between neighborhood water tanks.

## 2. Character Wardrobes & Style Profiles

Every contestant’s wardrobe visually encodes their heritage, personal discipline, and emotional defenses:

---

### 1. 金愛羅 (Kim Ae-ra / 愛羅) — *Oversized Korean Minimalist & Protective Neutrality*
* **Silhouette**: Boxy, relaxed, protective. Dropped shoulders, draped trousers, hidden silhouettes that deflect scrutiny.
* **Color Palette**: Muted neutrals—charcoal, oatmeal, washed olive, raw ivory, slate grey.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Oversized tailored wool blazer from an indie Seoul label.
  * High-waisted wide-leg pleated trousers with subtle drape.
  * Heavy-weight organic cotton boxy tees (no logos).
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Black leather fisherman sandals, canvas tote from an indie art bookshop in Mapo-gu, thin silver wire-rim spectacles, minimalist analog leather watch.

---

### 2. 佐佐木 蓮 (Sasaki Ren / 蓮) — *Japanese Heritage Workwear & Culinary Precision*
* **Silhouette**: Clean, utilitarian, structured. Clothes engineered for movement, durability, and kitchen craftsmanship.
* **Color Palette**: Indigo, off-white, raw canvas, French workwear blue, charcoal.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Pristine white heavyweight Japanese pocket tees (folded impeccably).
  * Indigo selvedge denim from Kojima, cuffs neatly rolled once.
  * Vintage herringbone twill French chore jacket (*bleu de travail*).
  * Washed charcoal linen half-apron tied securely around the waist during cooking.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Oiled leather Birkenstock Boston clogs in tobacco, polished silver signet ring with family crest, vintage Seki kanji pocket knife.

---

### 3. 舒米・巴奈 (Sumi Panay / 舒米) — *Indigenous Earth Botanicals & Fluid Organic Textures*
* **Silhouette**: Flowing, unconstrained, tactile. Handwoven textures, raw edges, and natural fiber movement that breathe with the body.
* **Color Palette**: Deep plant indigo, turmeric yellow, wild madder red, volcanic grey, undyed ramie hemp.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Hand-loomed ramie open-collar tunic dyed with mountain indigo.
  * Loose wide-leg organic cotton work pants tied with hand-twisted botanical cord.
  * Patchwork artisan vest woven with traditional Amis geometric tribal motifs.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Vegetable-tanned cross-strap leather sandals, cowrie shell necklace, carved driftwood hairpin, beaten silver ear cuffs.

---

### 4. 阮明珠 (Nguyen Minh Chau / 明珠) — *Deconstructed Upcycled Streetwear & Cyber-Punk Edge*
* **Silhouette**: High-contrast, dynamic, architectural. Spliced proportions, cropped tops over exaggerated oversized tailoring, exposed seams.
* **Color Palette**: Acid neon accents (lime, electric violet) against jet black, chrome silver, raw denim.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Deconstructed asymmetrical blazer crafted from two deadstock vintage suits.
  * Cargo pants refashioned with utility straps and contrast industrial stitching.
  * Upcycled mesh baby tees with custom screen-printed Saigon typography.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Chunky platform Salomon sneakers or Maison Margiela Tabi boots, stacked industrial silver rings, wired retro earphones, glossy berry lip tint.

---

### 5. 張凱文 (Chang Kai-Wen "Kai" / 凱) — *Taipei Indie Grunge & Manila Tropical Normcore*
* **Silhouette**: Slouchy, effortless, uncalculated. The wardrobe of someone who grabs whatever is on the floor and somehow looks naturally iconic.
* **Color Palette**: Sun-faded black, mustard yellow, vintage cream, plaid flannels, faded tropical prints.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Authentic sun-faded 90s indie band tees (Sonic Youth, Sunset Rollercoaster, No Party for Cao Dong, Eraserheads).
  * Relaxed carpenter jeans with faint oil stains from his vintage Yamaha motorcycle.
  * Open rayon camp-collar bowling shirts with Manila floral patterns.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Beaten-up black Chuck Taylor All-Stars with worn soles, silver braided rope chain, celluloid guitar pick tucked behind his ear.

---

### 6. 李諾亞 (Noah Lee / 諾亞) — *Tropical Architectural Linen & Technical Aerodynamic Performance*
* **Silhouette**: Tailored, streamlined, functional. Minimalist architectural precision blended with high-performance cycling engineering.
* **Color Palette**: Sandstone beige, sage green, graphite black, arctic white, deep sea navy.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Band-collar tailored breathable linen shirts with concealed horn buttons.
  * Technical 4-way stretch commuting trousers (Theory / Lululemon Lab).
  * Seamless aerodynamic merino wool cycling base layers (Rapha / Descente Allterrain).
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Triple-black On Running Cloudtilt sneakers or carbon road cycling shoes, matte titanium spectacles, Suunto titanium GPS sports watch, matte stainless steel thermos.
