# Aesthetic, Architecture & Fashion Guide: AERA

> *"A sharehouse is not just a backdrop; it is a glass terrarium designed to expose the friction between six different aesthetic worlds."*

## 1. Architectural Materiality: The Da’an Townhouse

The sharehouse is a meticulously renovated 1970s three-story Taiwanese townhouse (*tian-cuo* 透天厝) tucked deep inside the leafy lanes of Da’an District. The design preserves historic Taiwanese residential craft while incorporating Japanese minimalist joinery and modern industrial finishes.

```

│  Polished emerald- │  Embossed begonia  │  Custom 1970s geometric      │

```

### Room-by-Room Architectural Breakdown

#### Ground Floor: The Public Stage
* **Entrance Genkan (玄關)**: 
  * Sunken polished grey concrete. A clean bench of reclaimed camphor wood. 
  * Rows of clean matching leather guest slippers alongside the cast's daily shoes: Kai's beat-up Converse, Ren's oiled kitchen clogs, Mio's platform boots.
* **Open Chef's Kitchen & Dining Room**:
  * Massive central stainless-steel island with commercial-grade gas burners, overhead pot rack with copper pans, and dual sunken sinks.
  * Long 10-seater dining table crafted from a single slab of solid Taiwanese cypress (*hinoki* 檜木). Overhead hang three low-wattage fluted ceramic pendant lamps (warm 2400K light).
* **Sunken Living Lounge & Courtyard**:
  * Steps down into a sunken floor lined with original deep green-and-white flecked terrazzo (*mo-shi-zi*).
  * Low modular linen sofa in slate grey. No television. Bookshelves lined with art monographs, film stills, and architecture books.
  * Floor-to-ceiling iron-framed glass accordion doors open completely into the **Interior Courtyard**, where a 50-year-old banyan tree and giant elephant-ear leaves rise against raw concrete walls.

#### Second Floor: The Introspective Domain
* **The Studio / Library**:
  * Glass-walled room overlooking the courtyard. Contains Aera’s dual-monitor editing station, Yuhan’s drafting board, and a vintage Marantz turntable.
* **The Girls’ Room (3-Bed Dormitory)**:
  * Elevated tatami platform holding three individual low linen futon mattresses.
  * Sliding partitions of frosted rice paper and begonia-embossed glass (*hai-tang-hua*).
  * High clothing rack displaying Mio’s colorful statement jackets, Aera’s muted charcoal coats, and Yuhan’s architectural linens.

#### Third Floor: The Elements & The Guys’ Dorm
* **The Guys’ Room (3-Bed Dormitory)**:
  * Polished terrazzo floor with industrial steel-frame beds.
  * Wall racks holding Daichi’s carbon-fiber aero road bike, Kai’s vintage acoustic guitar case, and Ren’s framed culinary certification.
* **Rooftop Terrace & Laundry Pavilion**:
  * Weathered teak decking under a corrugated glass roof. Exposed aluminum ducting.
  * Two heavy industrial washing machines humming late at night.
  * String of warm tungsten festoon lights over a pair of Acapulco chairs and concrete planters filled with wild rosemary and mint. Views of the misty silhouette of Taipei 101 between neighborhood water tanks.

## 2. Character Wardrobes & Style Profiles

Every contestant’s wardrobe visually encodes their socio-economic background, personal discipline, and emotional defenses:

---

### 1. Kim Ae-ra (Aera) — *Oversized Korean Minimalist & Neutrality*
* **Silhouette**: Boxy, relaxed, protective. Dropped shoulders, draped trousers, hidden silhouettes that deflect scrutiny.
* **Color Palette**: Muted neutrals—charcoal, oatmeal, washed olive, raw ivory, slate.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Oversized tailored wool blazer from an indie Seoul label.
  * High-waisted wide-leg pleated trousers.
  * Heavy-weight organic cotton boxy tees (no logos).
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Black leather fisherman sandals, canvas tote from an indie bookshop in Mapo-gu, thin silver wire-rim spectacles, minimalist analog watch.

---

### 2. Sasaki Ren (Ren) — *Japanese Heritage Workwear & Culinary Precision*
* **Silhouette**: Clean, utilitarian, structured. Clothes engineered for movement, durability, and craftsmanship.
* **Color Palette**: Indigo, off-white, raw canvas, French workwear blue, charcoal.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Pristine white heavyweight Japanese pocket tees (folded impeccably).
  * Indigo selvedge denim from Kojima, cuffs neatly rolled once.
  * Vintage herringbone twill French chore jacket (*bleu de travail*).
  * Washed charcoal linen half-apron tied securely around the waist during cooking.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Oiled leather Birkenstock Boston clogs in tobacco, polished silver signet ring with family crest, vintage Seki kanji pocket knife.

---

### 3. Lin Ting-Yu (Mio) — *Avant-Garde Taipei Streetwear & Y2K Edge*
* **Silhouette**: High-contrast, dynamic, sculptural. Tight cropped tops paired with exaggerated oversized bottoms.
* **Color Palette**: Neon accents against jet black, chrome silver, acid wash, baby pink.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Deconstructed asymmetrical denim jacket designed by a Shih Chien classmate.
  * Low-rise parachute cargo pants with trailing utility ribbons.
  * Cropped baby tees with ironic Mandarin or English retro graphics.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Maison Margiela Tabi split-toe leather boots or chunky Salomon XT-6 sneakers, stacked silver rings, wired retro Apple earbuds (never AirPods), layered lip gloss.

---

### 4. Chen Yu-Han (Yuhan) — *Architectural Draping & Monochromatic Quiet Luxury*
* **Silhouette**: Elongated, elegant, structured. Architectural lines that convey authority, competence, and mature restraint.
* **Color Palette**: Monochromatic black, deep navy, stone beige, raw silk cream.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Band-collar black linen tunics with concealed plackets.
  * Flowing wide-leg culottes that billow gently in the summer draft.
  * Lightweight summer trench coat draped over shoulders when returning from site visits.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Pointed-toe leather mules, structured vegetable-tanned leather tote holding blueprint tubes, brushed brass architectural geometric earrings.

---

### 5. Chang Kai-Wen (Kai) — *Taipei Indie Grunge & Vintage Normcore*
* **Silhouette**: Slouchy, effortless, uncalculated. The wardrobe of someone who grabs whatever is on the floor and somehow makes it look effortlessly cool.
* **Color Palette**: Sun-faded black, mustard yellow, dirty white, plaid flannels, olive drab.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Authentic sun-faded 90s indie band tees (Sonic Youth, Sunset Rollercoaster, No Party for Cao Dong).
  * Relaxed carpenter jeans with faint oil stains from working on his vintage Yamaha motorcycle.
  * Open rayon camp-collar bowling shirts with vintage tropical prints.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Beaten-up black Chuck Taylor All-Stars with worn soles, silver braided rope chain, celluloid guitar pick tucked behind his ear.

---

### 6. Takahashi Daichi (Daichi) — *Technical Performance Athleticism & Executive Polish*
* **Silhouette**: Form-fitting, aerodynamic, hyper-functional. Zero excess fabric; communicates speed, discipline, and corporate control.
* **Color Palette**: Matte black, graphite, arctic white, performance navy.
* **Key Garments**:
  * Seamless technical merino wool athletic tees (Arc’teryx / Descente Allterrain).
  * Tailored 4-way stretch commuting chinos (Theory or Lululemon Lab).
  * High-performance windbreaker shell for morning Keelung River rides.
* **Footwear & Accessories**: Triple-black On Running Cloudtilt sneakers, Garmin Forerunner GPS multisport watch, titanium matte eyeglasses, black stainless steel insulated shaker bottle.
