* **Romantic Affinities**:

  * Repelled by performative masculinity, boasting, or corporate status-signaling.

### Key Relational Dynamos
1. **Aera & Kai (The Outsiders)**:
   * *Dynamic*: Late-night rooftop talks. Kai doesn't judge Aera's quietness, and Aera appreciates that Kai never performs for her. Instant organic chemistry that terrifies Aera because of how fast her walls drop.
2. **Ren & Mio (Fire and Ice)**:
   * *Dynamic*: Constant friction that masks mutual attraction. Ren thinks Mio is wasteful and loud; Mio thinks Ren is uptight and judgmental. When forced to cook together, their body language is intensely combustible.
3. **Daichi & Yuhan (The Competent Adults)**:
   * *Dynamic*: Effortless mutual respect over early morning espresso. They understand each other's career burnout, but both are too proud to ask for emotional vulnerability.
4. **Kai & Daichi (Spontaneity vs. Control)**:
   * *Dynamic*: The classic dorm roommate clash. Daichi's 5:00 AM alarm vs. Kai's 2:00 AM guitar noodling. Underneath the friction, Daichi secretly envies Kai's freedom, while Kai envies Daichi's focus.

## 4. The Production Village (Two Doors Down)

Tucked inside an empty ground-floor commercial space formerly used as a textile printing shop (No. 18):
* **The Control Bay**: A dark, air-conditioned room with black-out curtains. Three folding tables holding eight Sony reference monitors displaying all camera angles.
* **The Switcher Rack**: The digital core routing audio from the cast's wireless lavaliers and forty hidden high-gain condenser mics placed inside light fixtures, kitchen range hoods, and courtyard planters.
* **The Crew Desk**:
  * Maya sits at the front with an iPad tracking audience metrics, network Slack channels, and daily call sheets.
  * Chika stands near the backdoor with a helmet on her head and a walkie-talkie clamped to her chest.
  * Toby sits in the rear sound-isolated booth running two Avid editing suites, cutting the day's footage into broadcast reels.
