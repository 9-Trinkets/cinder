## 2. Key Cultural Phrases & Code-Switching

In the Da'an villa, the shared bridge language is **Mandarin Chinese**, but characters organically code-switch into Taiwanese, Korean, Japanese, Amis, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Thai, and English depending on their emotional stakes, comfort levels, or sensory memory.

### Taiwanese Mandarin Colloquialisms & Particles
* **真的假的 (Zhēn de jiǎ de?)**: *"Seriously? For real?"* Universal Taiwanese conversation starter used constantly by Kai and Minh Chau.
* **誇張 (Kuāzhāng)**: *"That's insane / over the top."* Used to react to everything from bistro prices to design deadlines.
* **蛤 (Hā / Há?)**: The reflexive Taiwanese exclamation when surprised or when asking someone to repeat themselves.
* **辛苦了 (Xīnkǔ le)**: *"Thank you for your hard work."* Said when someone finishes cooking, returns from a shoot, or clears the table.
* **沒事啦 (Méi shì la)**: *"It's nothing / don't worry about it."* Deflecting phrase used by Kai and Ae-ra when masking pain.
* **吃飽沒？ (Chī bǎo méi?)**: Taiwanese greeting: *"Have you eaten yet?"* Ren's quiet reflex when entering the kitchen.

### Korean Expressions & Emotional Markers (愛羅 Ae-ra)
* **대박 (Daebak)**: *"Incredible / Wow."* Slipped out by Ae-ra when tasting a surprising dish or viewing Sumi's textiles.
* **진짜 (Jinjja?)**: *"Really?"* Ae-ra's quiet reflex when listening intently.
* **괜찮아요 (Gwaenchanh-ayo)**: *"I'm okay / It doesn't matter."* Ae-ra's defensive reflex when overwhelmed.
* **그래 (Geurae)**: *"Right / I see."* A soft, rhythmic acknowledgment murmured during editing or late-night talks.

### Japanese Cultural Cues & Kitchen Terminology (蓮 Ren)
* **いただきます (Itadakimasu)**: Said before meals. Ren says this quietly every single time, even when tasting broth alone.
* **ごちそうさまでした (Gochisousama deshita)**: *"Thank you for the meal."* Always said to Ren by Ae-ra and Noah after dinner.
* **仕込み (Shikomi)**: Kitchen mise-en-place. Ren's morning sanctuary—his 6:00 AM rhythm.
* **こだわり (Kodawari)**: Obsessive, uncompromising dedication to craft or quality. Defines Ren's cooking and Noah's drafting.

### Taiwanese Indigenous (Amis) Expressions (舒米 Sumi)
* **Sa'icelen (撒依責愣)**: *"加油 / 拿出力量！"* Amis blessing and encouragement whispered before creative presentations.
* **Aray (阿賴)**: *"謝謝 / 感恩."* Spoken with a heartfelt hand over her heart when receiving kindness.
* **Malikuda (瑪莉庫達)**: Traditional circular singing and dancing; her memory of communal rhythm and grounding.
* **Orip (歐立普)**: *"生命 / 活著的氣息."* How Sumi describes plants that are ready for harvest and dyeing.

### Vietnamese Expressions & Sensibilities (明珠 Minh Chau)
* **Cà phê sữa đá**: Vietnamese iced milk coffee with sweetened condensed milk; Minh Chau’s morning fuel.
* **Dạ**: A gentle, respectful affirmative particle (*"Yes / I understand"*), slipping out when she speaks to elders or relaxes.
* **Trời ơi**: *"天啊！ / 我的天！"* Exclamation of disbelief when seeing dramatic runway garments or chaotic kitchen messes.
* **Cố lên**: *"加油！"* Spoken to Ae-ra when she is stuck on a late-night editing cut.

### Tagalog / Filipino Expressions (凱 Kai)
* **Kamusta**: *"你好嗎？"* Kai's easygoing, breezy greeting when entering a room.
* **Salamat**: *"謝謝."* Accompanied by his trademark nod and loose hand-on-heart gesture.
* **Tara**: *"走吧！ / 出發！"* Kai’s rallying cry when coaxing housemates to the rooftop for a drink or fresh air.

### Thai Expressions (諾亞 Noah)
* **Sabai (สบาย)**: *"舒服 / 愜意 / 放鬆."* Noah’s elusive ideal state of being that he constantly schedules but rarely feels.
* **Aroy (อร่อย)**: *"好吃！"* Said to Ren when a dish achieves perfect balance of acid and herbs.
* **Chai mai (ใช่ไหม)**: *"對吧？ / 不是嗎？"* Soft verbal punctuation when seeking agreement on architectural proportions.
